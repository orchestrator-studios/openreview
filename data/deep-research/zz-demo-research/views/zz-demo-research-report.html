Deep research

How this dashboard works

This page is a live, always-honest picture of one deep-research brief. Claude does the work through your chat; this page shows it happening and lets you check any claim against its sources.

The stops across the top

  • Brief — the question and the sub-questions Claude set out to answer, plus the scope.
  • Gather & verify — the work: sources gathered and read, findings drafted and verified, stage by stage, filling live.
  • Synthesis — the answer. A quality gate at the top (every sub-question answered, every finding cited and verified) sits above the findings, grouped by sub-question.
  • Sources — every source, searchable, each showing what cited it. It's where every count lands.

Every count is a link, every finding cites its sources

No number is a dead end — click it and the exact sources or findings open. Every finding lists the sources it rests on and carries a verification status, so you can follow any claim back to its evidence.

Generated from data/deep-research/zz-demo-research/ by tools/build_report.py — a projection of the underlying data, not hand-edited. Counts, citations and verification are read live from brief.json, sources.json and findings.json.
