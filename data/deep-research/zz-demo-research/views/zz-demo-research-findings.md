# Findings — Melatonin for adult sleep onset

_Generated from data by `tools/build_views.py` — do not edit by hand._

## Does melatonin reduce sleep-onset latency in healthy adults?
- Melatonin reduces sleep-onset latency by roughly 7 minutes versus placebo in healthy adults. _((corroborated · confidence high) — Ferracioli-Oda 2013, Zhdanova 2001)_

## What dose and timing are most effective?
- Lower doses (0.3-1 mg) taken 1-2 hours before bedtime are as effective as higher doses for sleep onset. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — Zhdanova 2001, Burgess 2010)_
- Over-the-counter melatonin content often varies substantially from the labeled dose. _((single-source · confidence medium) — Erland 2017)_

## Is short-term melatonin use safe in healthy adults?
- Short-term melatonin use shows no serious adverse events in healthy adults. _((single-source · confidence medium) — Besag 2019)_
- Over-the-counter melatonin content often varies substantially from the labeled dose. _((single-source · confidence medium) — Erland 2017)_
