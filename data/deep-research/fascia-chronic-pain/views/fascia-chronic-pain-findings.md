## By what mechanisms is fascia thought to contribute to chronic pain (innervation and nociception, inflammation/fibrosis, mechanotransduction, fascial densification/stiffness, sensitization)?
- Deep fascia (including the thoracolumbar fascia) is richly innervated with free nerve endings and nociceptors, and is often more pain-sensitive than the underlying muscle — establishing fascia as a genuine, primary source of nociception rather than an inert wrapping. _((corroborated · confidence high) — Front. Pain Res. 2025 — integrative MPS mechanisms model, Life (Basel) 2021 — thoracolumbar fascia nociception scope review, 2021, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther., Liptan 2010, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther. (hypothesis))_
- Fascial 'densification' — increased viscosity and packing of hyaluronan in the loose connective tissue between fascial layers — reduces the normal gliding of adjacent layers and is proposed to stimulate embedded nociceptors, forming a mechanical basis for myofascial pain. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — 2025 — fascia molecular level; Ca2+-hyaluronan mechanotransduction, 2019, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther. (fascial densification / hyaluronan), Life (Basel) 2021 — thoracolumbar fascia nociception scope review)_
- Fascia is mechanotransductive: mechanical strain drives fibroblast signaling (e.g. IL-6 secretion and a proposed Ca2+-hyaluronan feedback axis via Piezo1/TRPV4), coupling mechanical load to inflammation and matrix remodeling. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — 2025 — fascia molecular level; Ca2+-hyaluronan mechanotransduction, 2023, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther. (PMID 37330799), Front. Pain Res. 2025 — integrative MPS mechanisms model)_
- Fibrosis, thickening, and neuroinflammation in pathological fascia sensitize local nerve fibers and are hypothesized to feed central sensitization, linking peripheral fascial change to chronic, widespread pain. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — Front. Pain Res. 2025 — integrative MPS mechanisms model, Int. J. Mol. Sci. 2025 — fascia in CRPS narrative review, Liptan 2010, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther. (hypothesis))_
- Myofascial pain syndrome is increasingly framed as a disorder of the fascia (densification, altered innervation), and some authors propose it can progress toward fibromyalgia through connective-tissue changes ('fascial armoring') — a mechanistically plausible but speculative link. _((corroborated · confidence low) — Front. Pain Res. 2025 — integrative MPS mechanisms model, 2023, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther. (PMID 37330799), 2022 (PMID 35171940))_
- The mechanistic rationale for manual fascial therapies (e.g. Stecco Fascial Manipulation) is that deep friction raises tissue temperature and fragments densified hyaluronan, restoring gliding — a plausible mechanism supported by tissue studies, though distinct from proof of clinical efficacy. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — 2025 — fascia molecular level; Ca2+-hyaluronan mechanotransduction, 2019, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther. (fascial densification / hyaluronan))_

## What fascia-targeting treatments are used for chronic pain and what does the evidence say about their efficacy (manual/myofascial release, fascial manipulation, dry needling, hydrodissection, acupuncture, extracorporeal shockwave, exercise)?
- Myofascial release improves pain and physical function in chronic low back pain in meta-analysis, but shows no clear benefit for quality of life, pressure-pain threshold, or trunk mobility, and the overall evidence quality is low. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — Laimi et al. 2018, Clin. Rehabil., Front. Med. 2021 (meta-analysis))_
- The mechanistic rationale for manual fascial therapies (e.g. Stecco Fascial Manipulation) is that deep friction raises tissue temperature and fragments densified hyaluronan, restoring gliding — a plausible mechanism supported by tissue studies, though distinct from proof of clinical efficacy. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — 2025 — fascia molecular level; Ca2+-hyaluronan mechanotransduction, 2019, J. Bodyw. Mov. Ther. (fascial densification / hyaluronan))_
- Evidence for extracorporeal shockwave therapy in chronic recalcitrant plantar fasciitis is mixed: individual placebo-controlled RCTs conflict, but meta-analysis favors low-intensity ESWT over control for pain relief, and the treatment is generally considered safe. _((corroborated · confidence medium) — 2006 RCT (PMID 16818146), 2014 systematic review / meta-analysis, 2022 — ESWT and fascia nociceptors)_
- Across fascia-targeting treatments, the recurring conclusion is that they show promise but rest on a small, heterogeneous, low-quality evidence base — more rigorous, adequately powered RCTs are needed before firm efficacy claims are warranted. _((corroborated · confidence high) — Laimi et al. 2018, Clin. Rehabil., Front. Med. 2021 (meta-analysis), 2014 systematic review / meta-analysis)_
